**Description:**

Make a retained local/shared-project mismatch understandable and resolvable from
the ordinary compact editor. During final glyph review, the same-origin browser
correctly kept an older local test document and displayed its mismatch status.
Actions > Project > Agents and sync changed to Design, but the resulting visible
accessibility tree exposed no backup/use-shared controls. Preserve this observation
without claiming a diagnosed cause. A fresh isolated origin subsequently showed
the exact semantic project; that validates glyphs, not the recovery journey.

North star: NS-4, serving the Studio slice's existing recovery criterion. No
separate completion credit. Starting evidence: [current work](../WORK.md), the
current project protection/agent view and the private studio-project-lifecycle-02
browser captures. Do not remove local protection to make the notice disappear.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Reproduce a genuine retained local/shared mismatch on desktop and phone with
  exact independent project/draft pairs. Expose a clearly reachable recovery view
  from the notice and ordinary menu, including narrow/short layouts.
- Keep-local/pause-sync and backup/use-shared have clear outcomes, preserve the
  exact backup, and act on acknowledged project identities and revisions. Stale
  requests, failed backup and cancellation retain the current pair and history.
- Real browser and native journeys prove visibility, keyboard/focus, state and
  backup preservation. Semantic observation matches the chosen project after
  explicit admission. Include a return to the retained project; a fresh origin,
  cleared storage, compilation or a screenshot alone does not accept recovery.
