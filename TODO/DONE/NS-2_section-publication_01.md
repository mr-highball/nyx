# NS-2_section-publication_01 — Staged independent view sections

[North stars](../../MILESTONES.md) · [Task catalog](../README.md) · [Current work](../../WORK.md)

**Description:**

Supply a public Nyx prerequisite for retaining independent mounted sections while
other sections undergo structural publication. Existing whole-view Render and
strict retained TryRefresh do not offer a grouped section ownership boundary.
Keep the portable contract independent of LCL/DOM handles; use existing target
renderers through adapters. This prepares NS-4 authoring criterion 2 and NS-3
performance criteria 2/3. It does not replace their full Studio integration,
history, performance, accessibility or delivery criteria. Completion credit remains
pending; no extra north-star credit is assigned by introducing the prerequisite.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Typed section identity, managed change handles and an explicit lifecycle make
  prepared, published and rejected changes distinguishable. No caller document
  or mutable source tree is retained by a prepared target view.
- A grouped publication checks exact section revisions and refuses duplicate or
  stale sections before changing any physical placement. Unmentioned mounted
  sections retain their controls, runtime edits and event routes.
- Prepare or physical preview failure preserves accepted section ownership and
  reverses earlier previews without reconstructing their old controls. Publication
  occurs only after all previews succeed; retired candidates/owners disconnect
  their event and collection scopes.
- Native and HTTP browser actual-control journeys execute initial/multi-section
  publication, independent drafts/events, target failure rollback, stale/duplicate
  refusal and explicit retirement. Current desktop/narrow captures precede
  retirement; native checked heap evidence is scoped to this consumer.
- Public documentation states borrowed host/store/receiver lifetimes, extension
  obligations, revision scope, cancellation and the limits of view publication.
  It does not claim document Undo, Studio partitioning or speedup from this API.

**Blockers**

- [Portable owned model](NS-1_model_01.md) supplies independent realized trees.
